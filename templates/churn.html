Why the first version scored 1.0

The original label was "Recency over 90 days", and Recency was also an input, so every model read the answer off one feature. Now the label comes from a later period than the features, and the scores (about 0.71 to 0.75) reflect real predictive difficulty.

A baseline, not the final word

One cut-off date and a random split can't show whether the model works on a later period. My Risk Radar project takes the same problem further: two years of data, rolling snapshots, out-of-time testing and calibrated probabilities.
